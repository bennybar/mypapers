Privacy policy

My Papers · last updated 12 October 2026

What My Papers keeps

The receipts and documents you add (the files, and the details read from them: vendor, date, amounts, tags, text, notes). They are stored on your phone and, once you sign in, on the My Papers server (couchdb.ibarak.me) in a database that only your Google account can access, so they sync between your devices.

Google sign-in

Signing in with Google gives My Papers your Google account's email address and a stable account id. They are used only to identify you and give you access to your own database.

Gmail (optional, read-only)

If you connect Gmail, My Papers uses read-only access (gmail.readonly) to search your mailbox, on your phone, for emails with PDF or photo attachments and for emails that look like receipts. It lists what it finds for you to choose from. Only the items you choose are imported, as documents in your My Papers library. The list of found items stays on your phone. My Papers never sends, changes or deletes email, and does not store your emails on its server beyond the items you import.

My Papers' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to show you your receipts and documents and import the ones you pick; it is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train AI models, and not read by people.

AI reading (optional)

If you enter your own OpenAI API key, each document you add is sent to OpenAI to read its details, under your OpenAI account and OpenAI's terms. The key stays on your phone (encrypted) and is never sent to the My Papers server.

What My Papers doesn't do

No ads, no analytics or tracking, no selling or sharing of your data. Nothing is shared with anyone except the services you turn on above (Google for sign-in and Gmail, OpenAI for AI reading).

Deleting your data

Delete a document in the app and it is removed from your devices and the server. Signing out removes your papers from that phone. To delete everything stored for your account on the server, email bennybar@gmail.com. You can remove My Papers' access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

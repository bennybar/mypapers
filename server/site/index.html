My Papers

My Papers is a personal Android app for keeping receipts and documents. Scan a paper, share a PDF or photo from any app, or pick receipts out of your Gmail; the app reads each one (vendor, date, amounts, VAT) and tags it, and you can find any paper later with one forgiving search box.

Your papers sync between your devices through a private server, where each Google account has its own database that only it can access.

My Papers is a personal project, installed directly (not from an app store), for a small group of invited users.
